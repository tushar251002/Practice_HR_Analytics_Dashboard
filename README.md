# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze workforce data and understand employee attrition patterns.

The dashboard provides insights into employee demographics, attrition by department, age-group distribution, education fields, job satisfaction ratings, and gender-based attrition trends. It helps HR teams explore workforce patterns and identify areas that may require further investigation.

## Dashboard Preview

![HR Dashboard](Images/HR_Dashboard.png)

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition and workforce strength.
- Understand attrition patterns across departments.
- Explore employee distribution across different age groups.
- Examine attrition across education fields.
- Compare job satisfaction ratings across job roles.
- Explore gender-based attrition patterns across age groups.
- Present HR metrics through an interactive, easy-to-understand dashboard.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Workforce Overview
Displays key HR metrics, including total employees, attrition, attrition rate, active employees, and average age.

### 2. Department-Wise Attrition
Visualizes employee attrition across Research & Development (R&D), Sales, and Human Resources (HR).

### 3. Employee Distribution by Age Group
Compares employee counts across different age groups, including:
- Under 25
- 25–34
- 35–44
- 45–54
- Over 55

### 4. Job Satisfaction Analysis
Presents job satisfaction ratings across different job roles to help explore differences in employee experience.

### 5. Education Field-Wise Attrition
Compares attrition across education fields, including Life Sciences, Medical, Marketing, Technical Degree, Other, and Human Resources.

### 6. Gender-Based Attrition Analysis
Uses donut charts to compare attrition patterns by gender across different age groups.

### 7. Interactive Education Filters
Includes education-level filters that allow users to explore the dashboard for selected educational qualifications.

## Key Observations

Based on the dashboard:

- **Employee attrition:** 237 employees are recorded as having left, representing an attrition rate of 16.12%.
- **Active workforce:** 1,233 employees remain active.
- **Department distribution:** R&D accounts for 133 recorded attrition cases (56%), Sales for 92 (39%), and HR for 12 (5%).
- **Age distribution:** The 25–34 and 35–44 age groups represent substantial portions of the workforce shown in the dashboard.
- **Education fields:** Life Sciences and Medical have the highest displayed attrition counts among the listed education fields.
- **Job satisfaction:** Ratings vary across job roles, providing an opportunity to investigate differences in employee experience.

These observations describe the displayed data and do not establish the causes of employee attrition.
